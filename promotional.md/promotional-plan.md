## 1. Core Marketing Direction

### Main Positioning

Smart Digital is not only a digital shop. It is a local Assam-based digital service hub where people can get:

- AI tools for students, teachers, shops, businesses, and job seekers
- Website creation and WhatsApp automation
- PAN, Aadhaar, Voter ID, documentation, printing, scanning, and online form help
- Free tools like QR generator, ID card maker, games, and business website preview
- Assamese-friendly support and WhatsApp-based help

### Main Assam-Focused Message

Use this repeatedly:

> "Smart Digital brings useful AI and digital services to Assam in simple language, local style, and affordable pricing."

> "অসমৰ মানুহৰ বাবে সহজ, সুলভ আৰু স্থানীয় ডিজিটেল সেৱা - Smart Digital."

### Target Audiences

1. Teachers and private tutors  
   Promote: ExamCraft Pro, LipiAntar, QR Generator, ID Maker, main website services.

2. Students and job seekers  
   Promote: Resume Builder, LipiAntar, PatraLekhak, free tools, games.

3. Local shop owners and small businesses  
   Promote: BizWrite, ShopWrite AI, ScriptWala, QR Generator, Website Creation, WhatsApp Bot Automation.

4. Schools, coaching centres, clubs, NGOs, offices  
   Promote: ExamCraft Pro, ID Card & Certificate Maker, website creation, QR Generator.

5. Common people needing documentation help  
   Promote: PatraLekhak, offline services, PAN/Aadhaar/Voter ID/document services.

### Best Platforms for Assam

- WhatsApp Status: daily short promotions, before/after examples, free tool demos.
- WhatsApp Groups: teacher groups, business groups, student groups, village/local groups.
- Facebook Reels: best for local reach, teachers, parents, businesses, and CSC-type customers.
- Facebook Posts: detailed explanation posts, offers, testimonials, service lists.
- Instagram Reels: fast demos, student/job seeker content, shop owner content.
- Instagram Stories: polls, before/after copy, offers, quick CTAs.
- YouTube Shorts: reuse the same reels for longer-term discovery.

### Language Mix

Use different language styles depending on audience:

- Teachers: Assamese + English technical terms.
- Students: Assamese + Hindi/English mix.
- Shop owners: Simple Assamese, Hindi, and local English.
- Official/document services: Assamese + formal English.
- Instagram youth content: Hinglish + Assamese hooks.
