## 5. Featured case study — Jatiya Vidyalaya, Kachua Tiniali

slow, error-prone, and results took days to prepare.

generates the final weighted marksheet automatically.

**Outcome:** What used to be tallied by hand, exam by exam, is now one automatic calculation —
marksheets ready in minutes instead of days of manual work.

## 11. FAQ

**Do we pay every month?**
No. It's a one-time build cost plus a small yearly fee for domain, hosting, SSL and support
(₹15,000–₹20,000/year, revised 2026-09-04) — see /pricing/.

**Do we own everything?**
Yes — domain, data, code and every account are yours from day one.

**Does this work alongside government portals like Sewa Setu?**
Your records stay yours and can be exported at any time, so nothing stops you reporting to Sewa Setu
or any other government portal your school already uses. (Rewritten to a claim I can actually stand
behind — the original framing implied a specific integration I have no evidence exists. If Smart
Digital's systems do integrate directly with Sewa Setu today, tell me and I'll write the stronger,
more specific version.)

**How long does it take?**
Depends on scope — a business website is days; a school system follows the process in §6, discussed
and agreed up front in the written quotation.

**What if our internet is slow?**
The sites are built to be low-bandwidth friendly and work on an ordinary phone connection.

**Is everything available in Assamese?**
Yes — every page has a one-click Assamese/English toggle.

**Do you train our staff?**
Yes, in person, at your office, as part of every build.
