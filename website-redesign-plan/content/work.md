## /work/jatiya-vidyalaya/

**Headline:** Jatiya Vidyalaya, Kachua Tiniali
**Standfirst:** A student records and exam system that turned a multi-day manual marksheet process
into a few minutes of automated calculation.

**The problem**
Exam results were compiled by hand across four exams per term. Marks had to be weighted and totalled
manually before a marksheet could be issued — slow, and one arithmetic slip meant redoing a
student's sheet by hand.

**What we built**
A student records and exam system where office staff enter marks once per exam. The system applies
the school's weighting rules and generates the final marksheet automatically — one flow from marks
entry to a printable result.
