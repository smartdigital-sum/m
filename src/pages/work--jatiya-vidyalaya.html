Case study

Jatiya Vidyalaya, Kachua Tiniali

A student records and exam system that turned a multi-day manual marksheet process into a few minutes of automated calculation.

Working live website

The problem

Exam results were compiled by hand across four exams per term. Marks had to be weighted and totalled manually before a marksheet could be issued — slow, and one arithmetic slip meant redoing a student's sheet by hand.

What we built

A student records and exam system where office staff enter marks once per exam. The system applies the school's weighting rules and generates the final marksheet automatically — one flow from marks entry to a printable result.

The outcome

What used to be tallied by hand is now one automatic calculation — marksheets ready in minutes instead of days.
