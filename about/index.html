About Smart Digital

Web-app and software services, built and supported from Kampur, Nagaon — not outsourced, not remote-only.

Smart Digital is run by Suman Biswas, based in Kampur, Nagaon, Assam. We build websites and management software for schools, colleges and local businesses across the district — every project sat with, discussed, quoted and handed over in person.

Why local matters

We sit 3 km away. When something needs fixing, or your office staff need training, we show up — we don't route you through a ticket queue in another state.

Bilingual by default

Every page, in every product, in Assamese and English — not a translated afterthought, built in from the start.

Ownership

You own your domain, your data, your code, every account. One-time build, not a subscription.
