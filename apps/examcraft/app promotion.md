## Part 1 — Strategy

### 1. Sharpen the positioning first

Current tagline is generic ("AI Question Paper Generator"). Lead with the regional moat competitors don't have:

> **"Pure Assamese SEBA & AHSEC question papers in 30 seconds. Built in Assam, for Assam."**

Why it works:
- National tools (ChatGPT, Quillionz, Examify) **cannot do pure Assamese** — they leak Bengali. We spent real engineering on `ASSAMESE_LANGUAGE_PROFILE`. **That's the wedge.**
- SEBA/AHSEC syllabus alignment is local — teachers trust "made in Guwahati" over "made in Bangalore."

Use the national pitch ("CBSE/ICSE supported, English & Hindi too") as the **secondary** sell. Win Assam first, fund expansion from cash flow.

### 2. Fix the free-trial funnel leak

2 free demo papers + ₹30 single-paper pricing is a strong top-of-funnel. The leak: people generate the demo, see it works, then bounce because no one reminds them. Fixes:

- **WhatsApp opt-in on signup** ✅ shipped — see Part 3.
- **Day-2 follow-up template** (manual to start) — see Part 2D.

### 3. Channels ranked by ROI

| Channel | Cost | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp groups for teachers** (SEBA/AHSEC/CBSE, district-wise) | ₹0 | Highest intent. Share a 30-sec screen recording. One forward = viral in teacher circles. |
| **Facebook reels in Assamese** | ₹0–₹500/post boost | Teachers over 35 are heavy FB users in Assam. |
| **YouTube Shorts** (English + Assamese) | ₹0 | "How I made a class 10 paper without typing a word." |
| **School direct sales** | Time only | Walk into 10 nearby schools with a printed sample. Pitch the 100-pack (₹2,119) to the principal. 2 sales = ₹4,200 + recurring. |
| **CSC network** | Free, already have it | Other CSC operators = built-in resellers. Offer 20% commission on group/school packs. |
| **Google Ads** on "SEBA question paper class 10" | ₹100–300/day | Only after funnel is tight. Don't start here. |

### 4. Content plan (5 assets, reuse forever)

1. 60-sec demo video (Assamese voiceover): form → generate → PDF download.
2. Comparison post: "ChatGPT vs ExamCraft for Assamese papers" — show ChatGPT leaking Bengali.
3. Sample paper PDFs (Class 10 SEBA Math, Class 12 AHSEC Physics, Class 8 SEBA Science) — host on site, share as lead magnets.
4. Teacher testimonial video — give 1 friendly teacher the 15-pack free in exchange for a 30-sec phone-camera testimonial.
5. Pricing graphic: "What costs you 2 hours of typing now costs ₹30."

### 5. 30-60-90 day rollout

- **Days 1–30:** Make the 5 content assets. Join 20 Assam teacher WhatsApp/FB groups. Visit 10 local schools. **Goal: 20 paying users.**
- **Days 31–60:** Boost the best-performing FB reel (~₹3,000 total). Recruit 3 CSC operators as resellers. **Goal: first school pack sale.**
- **Days 61–90:** Add day-2 WhatsApp follow-up automation. Start small Google Ads (~₹200/day). **Goal: ₹15,000/month run-rate.**

### Main strategic tradeoff

Lead with the **Assamese moat** (slower national growth, but defensible and cheap to win) **vs.** positioning as **pan-India** (bigger TAM, but you compete with VC-funded tools and your ad spend dies fast).

**Recommendation: Assamese-first.** The code already proves we took it seriously. No one else has.

### Change 4 — WhatsApp custom-paper order consumes 1 credit + saves to Firestore

**Files:** `apps/examcraft/index.html` (new `#waOrderModal` + `.cpc-credit-note` line), `apps/examcraft/app.js` (rewritten `openExamCraftWhatsApp`, new `confirmWaOrder`, `closeWaOrderModal`, `collectExamCraftFormSnapshot`, updated `buildExamCraftWhatsAppMessage`), `apps/examcraft/style.css` (`.wa-order-*`, `.cpc-credit-note`)

The "Order via WhatsApp" button now ties WhatsApp orders to the user's pack credits and creates an audit trail. **Strategic shift:** WhatsApp orders are no longer bespoke/separately-priced — they consume 1 pack credit, same as a self-serve generation.

**New flow:**

1. **Not signed in** → toast: *"Please sign in first to order via WhatsApp"* + login modal opens.
2. **Signed in but no pack / 0 credits** → toast: *"You need an active pack to order via WhatsApp"* + switches to the Pricing tab.
3. **Signed in with credits** → green confirmation modal:
   > 📱 **Order paper via WhatsApp?**
   > Smart Digital will prepare your question paper based on the form details and send it back to you on WhatsApp.
   > **This will use 1 paper credit** (N credits remaining after this)
   > **[Cancel]** **[Confirm & Send →]**
4. **On confirm:**
   - Transactionally deducts 1 credit via existing `reservePaperCredit()` (dashboard updates immediately).
   - Saves a doc to new Firestore collection `whatsappOrders`:
     ```
     { orderId, uid, userName, userEmail, userPhone,
       form: { board, schoolName, cls, subject, chapters,
               totalQ, totalMarks, timeLimit, difficulty,
               medium, qtypes, instructions, imageSubject, imageClass },
       status: 'pending', creditDeducted: true,
       createdAt: serverTimestamp() }
     ```
   - Opens WhatsApp in a new tab with the **order ID + form details + account info** embedded in the message.
   - Modal switches to a green success screen showing the order ID.
5. **On error** (Firestore down, network fail, race condition) → credit auto-refunds via `releaseReservedPaperCredit()`, modal returns to confirm step, error toast shown. **No silent credit loss.**

**Order ID format:** `EC-{base36 timestamp}`, e.g. `EC-M3JKQA7C`.

**Sidebar copy** under the green WhatsApp button (all 3 languages):
> ⚡ Uses 1 paper credit from your pack.
> ⚡ আপোনাৰ পেকৰ পৰা ১টা পেপাৰ ক্ৰেডিট খৰচ হ'ব।

**Admin workflow (manual for now):**
- Open Firebase console → `whatsappOrders` collection → filter `status == 'pending'`.
- Build the paper using the form snapshot in the doc.
- Send PDF to customer on WhatsApp (matched by order ID, name, email, or phone).
- Manually set `status: 'delivered'` in Firestore (or `deliveredAt`).

**Edge case to know about:** If the user closes the WhatsApp tab without sending the message, the credit is still deducted (we can't detect tab-close). Acceptable cost — you can manually refund in Firestore via the user doc if anyone complains.

## Part 4 — Open Decisions (flag before scaling)

### D1. Opt-in checkbox: default unchecked

India's **DPDPA-2023** treats pre-checked marketing consent as invalid. The checkbox is **default unchecked**. If you want it default-checked (more opt-ins, weaker legal footing), flip `checked` on the `<input>` in `index.html` register panel.

### D2. Per-paper math: GST-inclusive

The earlier marketing draft used **₹19.7/paper** for the 15-pack — that was the pre-GST number. The app shows **₹23.33/paper** (₹350 ÷ 15). All copy in Part 2 has been updated to **₹23.33/paper** for consistency. **Don't revert to ₹19.7 in any new copy** — customers will catch the gap on the checkout screen and lose trust.

### D3. WhatsApp orders = 1 pack credit (positioning change)

Before Change 4, the WhatsApp card was positioned as "bespoke / custom — priced separately, manual quote on WhatsApp." After Change 4, **WhatsApp orders consume 1 pack credit just like a self-serve generation.** Implications you should be comfortable with before scaling:

- **You're trading per-order revenue for funnel simplicity.** A user who would have paid ₹100 for a bespoke 50-question paper now uses ₹23.33 worth of pack credit. Your margin per WhatsApp order drops.
- **Compensating gain:** WhatsApp is now a feature of the pack, not a separate negotiation. Pack value goes up → 15-pack and group/school packs become easier to sell.
- **Capacity risk:** If a school buys the 100-pack and decides to order all 100 via WhatsApp, you have to make 100 papers manually. Watch for this — add a soft cap or "premium" surcharge later if it happens.

If you want to keep some WhatsApp orders bespoke (e.g., for complex custom papers), one option later: add a checkbox in the order modal — *"Custom / non-standard request — quote separately, no credit used"* — that bypasses `reservePaperCredit()` and just opens WhatsApp with a special order tag.
